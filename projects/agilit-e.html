Project Agilit-e

Course project · Patent application filed

A narrow three-wheeled urban vehicle project focused on steering / suspension geometry and structural validation of the mobility system for a compact tilting platform.

DomainVehicle design
My focusSteering / structural analysis
ToolsFusion 360 + ANSYS
IPPatent application filed
ProblemBuildArchitectureValidationMediaStack

Problem

A narrow tilting vehicle must remain compact while transmitting steering, suspension and road loads through a lightweight structure. Geometry, stiffness and packaging therefore have to be considered together rather than as independent subsystems.

My contribution

  • Led modelling of the steering subsystem and its integration with the vehicle's suspension / mobility architecture.
  • Performed FEA-based structural analysis to identify critical stresses and validate the design under representative load cases.
  • Used the analysis to iterate component geometry before finalizing the course-project design.
