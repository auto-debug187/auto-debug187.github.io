Selected project

Project Vikram: Autonomous Ground Vehicle

Team Abhiyaan, IIT Madras · IGVC · 2025 – present

My work spans the vehicle's embedded drive interface and motion-control stack: reliable controller-to-motor communication, cascaded velocity control, and autonomous manoeuvres validated in ROS 2 / Gazebo.

RoleMechatronics engineer
NetworkCAN
ControlCascaded PID

ProblemBuildArchitectureValidationMediaStack

Problem

An autonomous ground vehicle needs a drive layer that is predictable enough for navigation: commands must reach each motor controller reliably, wheel velocity must track the requested motion, and higher-level planners need a stable interface rather than hardware-specific logic.

Contributions

CAN communication

Established CAN-based communication between the central controller and motor-control hardware for command delivery and telemetry exchange.

Velocity control

Implemented and tuned cascaded PID loops for stable wheel / vehicle velocity tracking, reaching sub-5% overshoot in validated tests.

Autonomous manoeuvres

Developed kinematic path-planning and parallel-parking logic and validated the behaviour in ROS 2, Gazebo and Nav2.

Human-aware signalling

Designed an event-aware pedestrian-alert horn subsystem for an autonomous golf-cart platform.

  • Parallel-parking and path-planning behaviour validated in simulation before vehicle integration.
