GPS-Denied UAV Navigation

NIDAR Drone Competition · Sep 2026 – present

An ongoing project exploring navigation in cluttered GPS-denied environments by combining high-resolution 2D LiDAR with vision-based scene information. This page is kept intentionally concise while the system is still under active development.

StatusOngoing
Primary sensor2D LiDAR
Secondary inputVision
FocusLocal planning / navigation
ProblemBuildArchitectureValidationMediaStack

Problem

Indoor and structurally complex environments remove GPS and often provide limited free space for aerial vehicles. The navigation stack therefore needs reliable local obstacle geometry and a planner that reacts quickly to newly observed structure.

Current direction

  • Fuse high-resolution 2D LiDAR obstacle information with vision-derived context.
  • Build a local planner suitable for GPS-denied clutter rather than relying on globally perfect maps.
  • Validate the stack first in simulation before transferring it to the competition vehicle.
