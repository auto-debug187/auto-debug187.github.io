Project CLIMB: Autonomous Pipe Inspection Robot

iBOT, IIT Madras · 2026 – present · Project Lead

A magnetic crawler for inspecting ferromagnetic pipelines and industrial structures. The project combines mechanical design, adhesion modelling, articulated mobility, ROS 2 simulation and an inspection stack for corrosion / thickness assessment.

Problem

Routine inspection of pipelines and large steel structures is difficult when access requires shutdowns, scaffolding or manual work at height. CLIMB is intended to carry inspection sensors while maintaining reliable adhesion as gravity direction and surface orientation change.

What I built / led

Mechanical architecture

Completed the crawler's articulated mechanical design and validated bending behaviour under operating loads. The articulation is designed to conform to changing geometry while preserving a stable central body for the inspection payload.

Magnetic adhesion

Modelled the magnetic circuit and simulated adhesion force in ANSYS Maxwell to check available normal force and guide magnet/yoke sizing before hardware manufacture.

Locomotion physics

Built a Gazebo model of the crawler to study wheel traction, magnetic preload, joint behaviour and gravity-dependent loading across horizontal and vertical surfaces.

Inspection stack

Developing a ROS 2 pipeline for visual corrosion inspection and non-destructive thickness sensing, with a digital representation of the inspected structure for repeatable condition tracking.
