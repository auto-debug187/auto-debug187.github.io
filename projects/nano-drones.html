GPS-Denied Nano Drones

Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0 · Drona Aviation · Bronze Medal

A GPS-denied flight-control project focused on extracting stable state estimates from onboard sensing and tuning the cascaded control stack enough to hold centimetre-scale indoor hover without external localization.

Problem

Small indoor drones cannot rely on GPS and have very limited sensing, compute and actuator authority. Small biases or poorly tuned loops quickly accumulate into drift or oscillation, so estimation and control must be treated together.

Approach

  • Implemented and tuned an EKF-based sensor-fusion pipeline for stable state estimation using the available onboard measurements.
  • Tuned cascaded flight-control loops and optimized the Magis firmware around the indoor operating envelope.
  • Iterated estimation noise, controller gains and firmware behaviour together rather than treating localization and control as independent problems.
