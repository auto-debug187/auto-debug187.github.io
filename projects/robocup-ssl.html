RoboCup Small Size League

Team Abhiyaan, IIT Madras · Jun 2025

A short but hardware-heavy project: centralized communication for a six-robot team and custom electronics for the high-power kicking mechanism.

Fleet6 robots
FocusNetworking + electronics
ArchitectureCentralized coordination
HardwareCustom PCB
ProblemBuildArchitectureValidationMediaStack

Problem

Small Size League robots require low-latency coordination while independently driving high-current actuators. The control system must distribute commands consistently to all robots and isolate logic-level electronics from the kicker's power stage.

Contributions

  • Engineered the communication topology and centralized networking framework for real-time coordination across six autonomous robots.
  • Designed the electrical subsystem and contributed to a custom PCB for powering and controlling the kinetic kicking mechanism.
