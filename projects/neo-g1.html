Project Neo-G1: Smart Neonatal Incubator

Sahaay, IIT Madras · Oct 2025 – Apr 2026 · Provisional patent filed

A low-cost modular incubator designed around safe thermal regulation, humidity control and practical electronics for resource-constrained healthcare settings.

ProblemBuildArchitectureValidationMediaStack

Problem

Neonatal incubators must maintain a tightly controlled environment while remaining safe under sensor faults, actuator faults and power variation. The project focused on achieving this with a modular, lower-cost architecture suitable for constrained settings.

Contributions

  • Designed the electrical and safety architecture, including sensor interfaces, actuator paths and PCB-level integration.
  • Developed closed-loop temperature and humidity control around the incubator's thermal dynamics.
  • Modelled the thermal/control system in MATLAB/Simulink before hardware tuning.
  • Worked under faculty supervision as part of Sahaay, IIT Madras; a provisional patent was filed for the project.
