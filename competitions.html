Competitions

A selection of technical competitions and engineering challenges spanning robotics, autonomous systems, controls and product development.

Caterpillar Tech Challenge 2026

Winner

Developed an RL-based adaptive PI control strategy for a time-varying plant, using TD3 to tune controller gains online under changing disturbance conditions. The final system reduced overshoot and undershoot from roughly 20--30% in the baseline controller to below 5% across tested cases, while reducing settling time from thousands of seconds to under 30 seconds.

Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0

Bronze Medal, 23 IITs | Drona Aviation Problem Statement

Worked on autonomous nano-drone flight in GPS-denied indoor environments. I implemented EKF-based sensor fusion, tuned cascaded flight-control loops and optimized the Magis firmware, achieving centimetre-level hover precision without relying on external localization infrastructure.

ISRO Robotics Challenge 2026

Top 20 of 600

Contributed to ASCEND, an autonomous aerial survey platform designed for feature detection and repeatable long-duration operation. I developed the DINOv2-based visual feature-localization pipeline with dense similarity matching, blur filtering, majority voting and spatial clustering, and also worked on the autonomous charging and docking system.

Hult Prize India Nationals 2026

Top 80 of 1,500+

Advanced to the India Nationals of the Hult Prize, a global student entrepreneurship competition. The experience focused on transforming a technical idea into a viable product proposition, including problem validation, business modelling, pitching and communicating impact to judges.

IGVC, Team Abhiyaan

Project Vikram

Worked on the autonomous ground vehicle stack for Team Abhiyaan's IGVC platform. My contributions included CAN-based communication between the central controller and motor hardware, cascaded PID velocity control, and kinematic path-planning and autonomous parallel-parking algorithms validated in ROS 2 and Gazebo.

RoboCup Small Size League

Team Abhiyaan, June 2025

Worked on the communication and electronics architecture for a six-robot autonomous football team. I contributed to centralized low-latency coordination between robots and designed parts of the electrical subsystem and custom PCB for the kinetic kicking mechanism.
